Chairless-chair exoskeleton

A passive exoskeleton that locks at the knee and sends your weight to the floor, so you can sit down anywhere. Course project, 2026. I did the concept, CAD, FEA, 3D printing and assembly.

  1. Walk, then sit

    The frame moves with your legs while you walk. When you sit back, the knee lock holds and the frame carries your weight down to the floor.

  2. Designed in SolidWorks

    I modeled the full assembly in SolidWorks, with every link sized to my own legs.

  3. Checked with FEA

    Static study with a 750 N load on aluminum 6061-T6.

    64.59MPa peak stress
    276MPa yield strength

  4. Buckling

    The legs work as columns, so I also ran a buckling study. Its first mode would need about 300 times the design load.

    304.88load factor, mode 1

  5. Printed and worn

    The prototype is 3D printed at full scale, braced with wooden diagonals and held on with straps. I tested it by wearing it around campus.

An AI agent that has to show its sources

At Mostla I'm building an agent that classifies company information. Every answer has to come with an exact quote from its source, and a deterministic verifier checks that quote before anything gets saved. No quote, no answer.
