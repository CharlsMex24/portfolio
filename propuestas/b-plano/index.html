Chairless-chair exoskeleton

A passive exoskeleton that locks at the knee and sends your weight to the floor, so you can sit down anywhere. Course project, 2026. I did the concept, the SolidWorks model, the FEA, the 3D printing and the assembly.

  1. 1Static load 750 N on aluminum 6061-T6.
  2. 2Peak von Mises stress 64.59 MPa, yield 276 MPa.
  3. 3First buckling mode at a load factor of about 300.
  4. 4Printed at full scale, wooden diagonals, strap mounts. Worn on campus.

An AI agent that has to show its sources

At Mostla I'm building an agent that classifies company information. Every answer has to come with an exact quote from its source, and a deterministic verifier checks that quote before anything gets saved.

Try it. The model proposes four answers, each with a quote. Only quotes that really appear in the source get through.

Source text

The frame was modeled in SolidWorks and checked with a static study with a 750 N load. The result was a 64.59 MPa peak von Mises stress, below the 276 MPa yield strength of aluminum 6061-T6. The printed prototype was tested by wearing it around campus.
