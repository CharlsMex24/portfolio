A chair you wear

A passive exoskeleton that locks at the knee and sends your weight to the floor, so you can sit down anywhere. Course project, 2026. I did the concept, the SolidWorks model, the FEA, the 3D printing and the assembly.

An AI agent that has to show its sources

At Mostla I'm building an agent that classifies company information. Every answer has to come with an exact quote from its source, and a deterministic verifier checks that quote before anything gets saved.

Python, web scraping, REST APIs, LLM tools, evaluation sets, tests.

Try the idea. The model proposes four answers, each with a quote. Only quotes that really appear in the source get through.
